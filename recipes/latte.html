Maple Sea Salt Latte

Description

A salty and sweet maple sea salt latte.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 or 1/4 cup steamed heavy cream or milk of choice
  • 2 shots brewed espresso
  • 2 teaspoons flaky sea salt
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • 1 teaspoon instant coffe

Steps

  1. Steam your milk of choice first. Brew the espresso and pour the espresso into a mug.
  2. Add the flaky sea salt to the brewed espresso and stir.
  3. Add the maple syrup to the brewed espresso and stir.
  4. Pour in the steamed milk and combine with the espresso.
  5. Top with a teaspoon of instant coffee and more flaky sea salt, sip, and enjoy!
