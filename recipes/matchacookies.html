Matcha Cookies

Description

Sweet and earthy matcha cookies with white chocolate chips.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 2 teaspoons matcha powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup vegan butter softened to room temperature
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons ground flaxseeds
  • 3 tablespoons oat milk or soy, almond
  • 1 cup vegan white chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F and line two cookie sheets with silicone mats or parchment paper
  2. In a small-medium bowl, sift the flour, cornstarch, matcha, and baking soda. Add the salt and stir well to combine. Set aside.
  3. In the bowl of a stand mixer with the paddle attachment, or using a handheld mixer, beat the softened vegan butter and sugars until creamy. Mix in the vanilla, flaxseeds and milk.
  4. Gradually add the flour mixture, mixing on low speed until just combined.
  5. Mix in the white chocolate chips. You may need to use your hands to incorporate them.
  6. Using a cookie scoop or spoon, roll into balls about 1 1/2 tablespoons each. Place on the prepared pans and bake for 10-12 minutes, until the edges are just set.
  7. Let cool on the pans for 5 minutes, then transfer to a cooling rack. Enjoy!
